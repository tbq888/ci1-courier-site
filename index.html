CI1 Courier

CI1 Courier is a small personal tool used only by its developer. It runs on the developer's own computer, reads the list and contents of a few of the developer's own Google Drive folders, and sends short status notifications to the developer's own phone. It has no user accounts, no sign-up and no public service.

CI1 Courier 是开发者本人自用的小工具，只在开发者自己的电脑上运行：读取开发者本人几个 Google Drive 文件夹的列表与内容，并把简短的状态通知发到开发者本人的手机。不对外提供服务。
